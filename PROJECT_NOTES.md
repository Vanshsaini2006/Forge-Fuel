# Project presentation notes — Forge Fuel

## One-minute explanation

Forge Fuel is a fictional direct-to-consumer sports nutrition storefront. The website targets beginner and intermediate gym-goers who often face an overwhelming number of supplement choices. Its visual design is bold and product-led, while the navigation is organised around common shopping categories: protein, creatine, pre-workout, wellness, fit foods and accessories.

## Target audience

- Primary: 18–30-year-old students and early-career professionals who train at a gym and shop online.
- Secondary: casual fitness enthusiasts who want convenient fit foods and simple accessories.
- Typical needs: discover products quickly, compare prices, understand product categories, find entry-level items and see delivery/offer details.

## Digital marketing objectives

1. **Awareness:** memorable visual identity, a clear hero message and campaign line.
2. **Consideration:** category pages/filters, pricing comparisons, product quick views, ratings placeholders and FAQ content.
3. **Conversion:** clear calls to action, add-to-cart, shipping threshold, demo coupon, saved products and checkout flow.
4. **Retention:** newsletter signup concept, saved products and a repeatable brand voice.

## Website features to demonstrate during evaluation

1. Click a category card; the product catalogue filters to that category.
2. Search for “protein” or “oats”.
3. Sort products by price or rating.
4. Open Quick View, then add an item to the bag.
5. Increase/decrease quantity and remove an item.
6. Enter `FORGE10` in the cart to show demo discount handling.
7. Open Saved Products (heart icon) to see wishlist interaction.
8. Choose a goal in “What are you working towards?” to filter the catalogue.
9. Submit the newsletter form and show the local confirmation.
10. Continue to the simulated checkout and note that it intentionally does not process payments.

## Potential performance metrics for a future real campaign

- Product list click-through rate
- Search usage and no-result rate
- Product detail view-to-add-to-cart rate
- Cart abandonment rate
- Checkout completion rate
- Coupon redemption rate
- Email signup conversion
- Repeat purchase rate

These are suggested metrics, not measured results; the prototype contains no analytics integration.

## Improvements if the project scope expands

- Replace CSS package illustrations with licensed product/athlete photography.
- Create individual category/product pages and add product comparison.
- Add a server-side catalogue, stock and order management.
- Add a secure payment gateway only in a real, reviewed implementation.
- Add verified labels, third-party testing evidence and compliant claims.
- Use privacy-respecting analytics and a functioning email marketing platform after obtaining appropriate consent.
